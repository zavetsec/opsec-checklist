What is OPSEC — and why it matters
OPSEC (Operational Security) is a systematic approach to protecting information about yourself — your data, actions, intentions, and associations. The core idea is not paranoid rule-following but deliberate control over what others know about you. You decide who gets access to what, and how much.
We live in an era where surveillance has become the default. Every smartphone is a tracker in your pocket. Every browser session builds a dossier on its owner. Banks, social platforms, marketplaces, ISPs, smart devices, and governments collect, store, and analyze your data continuously. Ad networks monetize every click. Data brokers sell your personal profile to anyone who pays. Criminals harvest breach databases. In a fully digitized world, complete anonymity is nearly impossible — but meaningfully shrinking your attack surface, closing obvious vectors, and raising the cost of targeting you to unacceptable levels is real and achievable.
